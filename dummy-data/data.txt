# Acme Corporation - Employee Handbook & HR Policies
## Last Updated: January 2026

---

## 1. WORKING HOURS AND ATTENDANCE

### Standard Work Hours
- Core business hours: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday
- Flexible work arrangement: Employees may adjust start times between 7:00 AM and 10:00 AM with manager approval
- Lunch break: 1 hour, typically between 12:00 PM and 2:00 PM

### Remote Work Policy
- Hybrid work model: Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week
- Remote work requests must be submitted 48 hours in advance
- Employees must be available during core hours (10:00 AM - 3:00 PM) regardless of location
- Home office equipment allowance: $500 one-time stipend for new employees

### Time Tracking
- All non-exempt employees must clock in and out using the TimePro system
- Overtime must be pre-approved by direct supervisor
- Overtime rate: 1.5x regular pay for hours exceeding 40 per week

---

## 2. PAID TIME OFF (PTO)

### Annual Leave Allocation
- 0-2 years of service: 15 days per year
- 3-5 years of service: 20 days per year
- 6-10 years of service: 25 days per year
- 10+ years of service: 30 days per year

### Sick Leave
- 10 paid sick days per year
- Unused sick days do not roll over
- Medical documentation required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days

### Holiday Schedule
Acme Corporation observes the following paid holidays:
- New Year's Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Presidents' Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day and day after
- Christmas Eve and Christmas Day

### Parental Leave
- Primary caregiver: 16 weeks paid leave
- Secondary caregiver: 6 weeks paid leave
- Adoption leave: Same benefits as birth parents

---

## 3. COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

### Pay Schedule
- Employees are paid bi-weekly on alternating Fridays
- Direct deposit is mandatory for all employees
- Pay stubs available through the HR Portal

### Health Insurance
- Medical, dental, and vision coverage available
- Company covers 80% of employee premiums
- Company covers 50% of dependent premiums
- Open enrollment period: November 1-30 each year

### 401(k) Retirement Plan
- Employees eligible after 90 days of employment
- Company matches 100% of contributions up to 4% of salary
- Additional 50% match on contributions between 4-6%
- Immediate vesting for employee contributions
- Company match vests over 3 years (33% per year)

### Professional Development
- Annual training budget: $2,000 per employee
- Tuition reimbursement: Up to $5,250 per year for approved programs
- Conference attendance: 2 approved conferences per year

---

## 4. CODE OF CONDUCT

### Workplace Behavior
- All employees must treat colleagues with respect and professionalism
- Harassment, discrimination, and bullying are strictly prohibited
- Violations should be reported to HR or through the anonymous ethics hotline

### Dress Code
- Business casual attire is standard
- Client-facing meetings may require business professional attire
- Casual Fridays: Jeans and company-branded apparel permitted

### Confidentiality
- All company information is confidential unless explicitly stated otherwise
- NDA agreements must be signed upon hiring
- Violation of confidentiality agreements may result in immediate termination

### Social Media Policy
- Do not disclose confidential company information on social media
- Personal social media use should not interfere with work responsibilities
- Identify personal opinions as your own, not the company's

---

## 5. PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT

### Performance Reviews
- Annual performance reviews conducted in December
- Mid-year check-ins conducted in June
- 360-degree feedback incorporated for management positions

### Performance Improvement Plans (PIP)
- PIPs may be initiated when performance does not meet expectations
- Standard PIP duration: 60-90 days
- Regular check-ins required during PIP period

### Promotion Criteria
- Minimum 12 months in current role before promotion eligibility
- Demonstrated mastery of current responsibilities
- Manager recommendation required
- Available budget and headcount considerations

---

## 6. WORKPLACE SAFETY

### Emergency Procedures
- Fire evacuation routes posted on each floor
- Quarterly fire drills conducted
- Emergency contact: Security desk at extension 9999

### Workplace Injuries
- Report all injuries immediately to supervisor and HR
- Workers' compensation coverage provided
- Incident reports must be filed within 24 hours

### Health and Wellness
- On-site gym available to all employees
- Mental health resources through Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
- Annual flu shots provided free of charge

---

## 7. TERMINATION POLICIES

### Voluntary Resignation
- Two weeks' notice expected for non-management positions
- Four weeks' notice expected for management positions
- Exit interview required

### Involuntary Termination
- At-will employment state; employment may be terminated at any time
- Severance: 2 weeks' pay per year of service (minimum 2 weeks, maximum 26 weeks)
- COBRA benefits information provided upon termination

### Return of Company Property
- All company property must be returned on last day of employment
- Includes: laptop, ID badge, parking pass, keys, and any company documents
- Final paycheck withheld until property returned

---

## 8. EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT

### Travel Expenses
- Air travel: Economy class for flights under 6 hours
- Hotels: Up to $200 per night in standard markets; $300 in high-cost cities
- Meals: Up to $75 per day (receipts required)
- Ground transportation: Reasonable taxi/rideshare expenses covered

### Business Expenses
- Submit expense reports within 30 days of expense
- Manager approval required for expenses over $100
- Finance approval required for expenses over $500
- Reimbursement processed within 2 pay periods
